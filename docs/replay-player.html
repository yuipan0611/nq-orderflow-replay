Replay validation

Level 1 supplies the best bid, best ask, and their sizes. Level 2 adds, updates, and removals are applied by side and price. The replay shows observed levels up to nine ticks from the best quote on each side.

A trade at the preceding best ask is inferred as buy-initiated. A trade at the preceding best bid is inferred as sell-initiated. Other trades retain an unknown side.
